01 About

Learning by building

I'm Fırat Yaman. I'm interested in software development in general, and most of my attention goes to web development and Android applications.

I like experimenting with different technologies: picking something new, building a small thing with it, and keeping what turns out to be useful.

This site is a good example. It started in June 2020 as a single page with my name and a few social links, and it has now been rebuilt as a small, dependency-free portfolio that can grow as I publish more work.
